Short: V12 emotion unleashed.

Full:
The Ferrari 12Cilindri Spider represents the purest expression of Ferrari’s engineering philosophy — a naturally aspirated V12 combined with an open-top driving experience. This model is ideal for enthusiasts seeking authenticity, emotion, and performance in its most refined and uncompromising form.
